Our story

Brewed slow. Poured with love.

Honeydrop started with one stubborn idea: that a cup of milk tea should never be an afterthought. We steep our teas in small batches, cook our tapioca fresh through the day, and sweeten everything with real cane and brown sugar — never syrups from a jug.

Whether you're a classic-black-tea loyalist or chasing this season's fruit blend, there's a drink here with your name on it.
