Using honey safely

  • No honey under age 1. Honey can cause infant botulism — never give it to babies younger than 12 months.
  • Not a replacement for medical care. These are traditional comfort remedies. See a doctor for high fever, trouble breathing, severe pain, or symptoms that last or get worse.
  • Don't boil honey. Add it to warm (not boiling) liquids to keep its beneficial enzymes.
  • Allergies & conditions. Skip anything you're allergic to, patch-test skin remedies, and check with your doctor if you're pregnant, diabetic, or on medication.
  • Wounds & burns. Only use honey on minor cuts/burns; seek care for deep, large, or infected wounds.
